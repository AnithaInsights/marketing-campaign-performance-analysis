# Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes marketing campaign data to understand campaign performance and identify factors that influence ROI, conversion rates, customer engagement, and overall campaign effectiveness.

The analysis was performed using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall marketing campaign performance
- Compare ROI across companies, channels, and locations
- Analyze conversion rates across different campaign types and languages
- Understand customer segments and target audiences
- Examine the relationship between acquisition cost and ROI
- Analyze engagement levels across campaigns and channels
- Identify time-based trends in conversion rate and engagement
- Explore geographic differences in campaign performance
